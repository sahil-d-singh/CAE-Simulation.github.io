Simulation & Analysis Projects

LS-Dyna · ANSYS

Li-Ion Battery Enclosure — Structural & Safety Analysis

  • Modal analysis ensured all structural components maintained first natural frequency above 50 Hz (road excitation), preventing resonance under vehicle operating loads.
  • Nail penetration test simulated in LS-Dyna using Electro-Mechanical-Thermal coupling to assess thermal runaway risk and validate enclosure design.
  • 10G static loading stress analysis and full crash-load analysis performed to verify structural integrity under extreme boundary conditions.
LS-Dyna ANSYS Crash Analysis Modal Analysis FEA

MATLAB · Simulink · Simscape

Vehicle Dynamics & Control Systems Modeling

  • Modelled an inverted pendulum on a cart system; conducted design studies comparing standard state-space control with Kalman Filter (KF) and Extended Kalman Filter (EKF) observer designs to evaluate estimation accuracy under noisy sensor conditions.
  • Derived equations of motion for a double spring-mass-damper vehicle dynamics model; applied ODE45 to numerically solve and plot time-domain velocity and displacement responses.
  • Applied Laplace and Transfer Function methods with Bode plot analysis to assess system stability margins and frequency-domain characteristics.
MATLAB Simulink Kalman Filter EKF Control Systems Simscape

ANSYS · NX · HyperMesh

Starter Motor Bracket — FEA Stress & Design Optimization

  • ANSYS software used to simulate actual loadings on a starter motor bracket. Static structural analysis performed with 30G loading applied independently in all three directions to evaluate stress concentrations across meshed regions.
  • Cranking moment impact loads assessed; ANSYS maximum von Mises stress of 179 MPa confirmed below the 200 MPa allowable threshold, validating design safety.

Redesign the bracket to reduce the stress by at least 10%

  • HypermMesh software used to simulate 500 N loadings on a bracket. Static structural analysis were performed and regions with high stress were identified .
  • Redesigned the bracket geometry in NX using spline-based fill surfaces to add material at high-stress corners and eliminate sharp stress risers — achieving a 35% stress reduction (from ~650 MPa to ~526 MPa) while maintaining original gauge thickness. Mesh convergence study conducted across seven refinement levels (DOF 6,480 → 21,882)..

MSC ADAMS · PTC Creo

Engine Valve Train — Kinematic & Dynamic Analysis

  • Investigated the Cylinder Activation & Deactivation System (CADS) working mechanism using MSC ADAMS. PTC Creo 3D para-solid model imported to ADAMS with all connections, contacts, and forces defined.
  • Time-based simulation conducted using angular displacement as input (engine RPM not directly accepted); body torque and displacement analyses performed across the operating range to validate CADS kinematics and contact force behavior.
MSC ADAMS PTC Creo Multi-Body Dynamics Kinematic Analysis Contact Forces

GT-Suite · GT-Power

Engine Friction Torque & Lubrication System Simulation

  • GT-Power 1D simulation used to model engine friction-torque behavior. Friction contributions from piston rings (first, second, oil), skirt, and main bearings calculated individually; total engine friction torque (Tf) derived as the summation.
  • Boundary condition-based simulation executed from GT-Power's built-in engine friction model; cam bush and gear train friction excluded as negligible contributors for this study scope.
  • Modelled a complete engine lubrication system and executed a Design of Experiments (DOE) to characterize sensitivity of oil pressure and flow distribution to key design parameters.
